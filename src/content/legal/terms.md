Calendar Sync is optional. An authorized merchant user must connect a Google Account through Google’s OAuth process and select the calendar that will receive restaurant reservations. You must have permission to use that account and calendar and to send any reservation information included in events.

Sentio POS accesses the calendar list and events created by Sentio POS to provide this feature. Its access, storage, and use of Google data are explained in our Privacy Policy.

You are responsible for choosing an appropriate calendar, checking its sharing permissions, and reviewing event information. People with access to the selected calendar may be able to see reservation details.

You can select **Disconnect** on the **Calendar Sync** page to revoke the token and delete stored calendar links. **Events already created in Google Calendar remain there**, and must be managed or deleted directly in Google Calendar. You can also revoke access through [Google Account Permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Revoking or disconnecting access stops future synchronization through that connection. It does not cancel restaurant reservations in Sentio POS or delete your Sentio POS account. Google Calendar availability, permissions, quotas, and changes to Google’s services can affect synchronization. Check important reservations in Sentio POS and review the selected calendar for successful event creation.

Google Calendar is supplied by Google and is subject to Google’s applicable terms and policies. Sentio POS is an independent product; use of the integration does not imply Google endorsement.
